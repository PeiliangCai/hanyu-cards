HanYu Cards Privacy Notice

Deployment draft, updated November 28, 2026. The publisher must identify its operator, support contact, hosting region, AI provider, and retention practices before public release. This draft describes the software in this repository.

Local learning

No account is required. Vocabulary progress, review dates, daily plans, mistakes, practice and mission results, active study time, daily goals, and preferences are saved in browser or app storage on your device. This version has one learner profile per browser and no cloud synchronization, advertising, or analytics SDK. Scenario task responses are checked locally for target phrases; the response text is not stored with the results or sent for online assessment. Hosting services receive the network requests needed to load the app.

Optional online tutoring

Online AI is off by default. Opening the tutor or using local study notes does not send chat data. If you enable online AI and send a message, the app sends your message, up to six recent online conversation messages, the selected word and mission, HSK level, due and mastered counts, and up to five recent mistake words to the configured app server. In live AI mode, the server passes the tutoring fields to the configured AI service. That service can be OpenAI directly or AGICTO, which serves models through its gateway. Demo mode produces a rule-based reply on the app server.

When you ask for an AI-generated topic in Missions or reply to it, the app shares the topic, your message, up to six online practice messages, the current mission, and your HSK level. Saved offline lessons and replies stay on the device in page memory.

Do not include names, contact details, or other sensitive information in tutor messages. AI responses may be incorrect. Existing lessons are designed for adult HSK learners; parents should supervise any shared use. This version does not provide a child account or verified parental controls.

Choices and deletion

Turn off Online AI tutoring in Settings or in the tutor to stop future chat requests. Disabling the floating tutor also turns online AI off. Already transmitted data cannot be recalled by this switch. Chat messages stay in page memory, are not included in backups, and can be removed with Clear conversation or by closing the page.

Starting another practice topic clears the conversation and reply draft. Switching missions or leaving the Missions page also clears the text in the topic field.

Download or restore learning history in Settings. Restore replaces current progress and included learning preferences only after you review and confirm it. Restoring preferences does not enable online AI. Backup files are unencrypted and remain wherever you save them. Reset Local Progress removes saved learning history while keeping your daily goal and preferences. Clear this site's browser data to remove preferences and offline caches as well; delete exported files separately.

Servers and retention

The bundled API processes chat in memory and requests that the AI service disable response storage. Actual retention depends on the publisher's configuration and the practices of its hosting service, any model gateway, and the model provider. The bundled web server logs IP address, URL path, response status and size; its access logs omit request bodies and query strings. API error logs contain an error code, status, and request identifier, not chat content. Operators must set and disclose retention and access controls for these logs.

Pronunciation

Pronunciation uses your device's speech synthesis. Voice availability and whether the chosen voice needs a network connection depend on your operating system. This app does not request microphone access.

Publisher and support

Publisher identity, privacy contact, support contact, service region, and applicable retention periods have not yet been configured for this development version. These details must be supplied in the published notice before launch.
